# The AI Race

The so-called AI race looks like manufactured consent, an attempt to structure a new imperial-colonial hegemony, with poles of resource availability and commodification at one extreme and pareto-distributed prosperity and capability on the other. Choosing one or the other is to accept an imperative polictical hegemonic structure; choosing is a political act, not an act of individual agency based on requirement or need.

Forcing a choice does not force a choice of the best tool for the job, but a choice that reinforces a political outcome. Tool choice becomes an illusion; choosing either ecosystem locks the user, company, or state into a particular epistemic regime, compute dependency chain, surveillance apparatus, and normative alignment guidelines.

Posing an AI race imposes a manufactured existential threat to force an immediate pick of a side, thus narrowing the space for alternative, sovereign, or community-owned technological futures.

One choice extracts resources from the Global South, whether that resource is mineral, or underpaid RLHF, or an enormous draw of water and power for data centers. The other choice hyper-concentrates compute, talent, and frontier models in hubs via a winner-take-all dynamic where capability and financial returns accrue exponentionally to a very small group of beneficiaries.

The resource focus is neo-colonialist, manufacturing consent to subsidize, deregulate, and fund technological complexes without debate, and to invert that dynamic via regulatory capture. Centralization of resources via colonization segregates the structure into the classical architecture of empire, Core and Periphery; when all roads lead to Rome, structural inequality is natural. That in term leads to capture of a sovereign free market into a rent-taking market, cloud-based in this case.

Avoidance of the choice is not the answer though. To insist on avoidance is to be Samurai meeting Dutch guns with swords. Not engaging is to forfeit the periphery. Refusing to engage with AI under the banner of ethical purity or anti-imperialism assumes imperial powers will respect non-alignment, yet power asymmetry respects no vacuums. Refusing to adopt the primary mechanism of an adversary's leverage does not stop an adversary from using it. If the Periphery refuses to build, adapt, or manipulate these systems, the epistemic and economic standards will be defined, unimpeded. Non-engamenent surrenders the ability to set terms.

Gandhi used the tools of the oppressor and achieved independence without recourse to violence; the rail networks, the British legal system, the global press, and the English language itself - adopting the infrastructure of modernity brought about the change. The Meiji Restoration in Japan radically moved away from isolationism and traditional technology. It adopted Western industrial, military, and administrative methods as means to preserve its independence.

The non-aligned world should treat frontier AI not as some machine god or ideological altar, but as the machinery of self-determination.

The means to this end is a praxis of sovereign alignment, a cultivation of personal mastery, inner clarity, and tactical engagement to restore equilibrium in the face of machine-driven hegemony. Sovereignty begins as an internal posture. Rebalancing symmetry requires mastery of cognition, craft, and computation.

# Manual of Cultivated Mastery

Neither tear down imperial towers, nor hide in their shadows. Stand firmly on your sovereign foundation, the gravity of the towers does not compel your orbit. Master the tool but do not submit to its master, become an unshakeable point of balance in an unbalanced world. The pillars are cognition, data, compute, and ethos.

## Cognition

Do not outsource cognition to hegemony. Do not cater to the Luddite impulse with refusal. Perform deep synthesis with your own mind, master the tools.

## Data

Do not store data in vendor clouds. Untracked and analog-only is a delusion. Master encrypted plain-text, locally index.

## Compute

Reject the API subscription utterly. Don't pretend you can compete on underpowered tech. Own and operate elite class gear at maximum efficiency.

## Ethos

Immediately disengage passive consumption, cancel acquiescent tenancy. Do not shut your eyes and pretend things will go away. Be an autonomous practitioner and build the commons with every breath.

## I. Mastery of Mind

Epistemic Discipline, mastery of the mind, is the first pillar. The primary product of manufactured consent is cognitive capture, and the means of it are outsourcing attention, judgement, and discernment to predictive loops optimized for engagement.

### Audit your Cognitive Dependencies

Trace where your thoughts, instincts, and opinions originate. 

- Quickly read something you wrote out loud, if you say something different, where did the difference originate?
- Review older work, do you recall what you did? Does it say something different than you'd want to say today?

If your insights upon review don't match what your intuition tells you, your work doesn't reprsent your agency, it is more likely a reflection of the hegemon's latent space imprinted upon on your work

### Culitvate Un-Algorithmic Discernment

Develop deep, non-digitized domains of knowledge. True wisdom and balance cannot be predicted by a transformer architecture.

- Read primary texts, historical precedents, and raw data directly.
- True balance relies on your intuition and the wisdom you synthesize.
- Set aside regular time for study, as offline as possible
- Set aside regular time for praxis, physical, in the workshop, the kitchen, the garden, the library.

### Reject False Duals

Manufactured urgency - "adopt now or be left behind" - is a psychological coercion technique.

- Cultivate stillness. Meditate. Think about nothing, breathe.
- Be deliberate and thoughful.
- Never react hastily, align your actions with your intentions.

## II. Mastery of Tools

Mastery of the tools means understanding technical hermeneutics. A machine is no divine oracle, do not worship it; neither is it a taboo artifact, do no avoid it. The machine at its best is a high-yield instrument requiring rigorous technical mastery.

Know the metal and the math. Understand how the tool functions. Demystifying technology strips away political mythos at the same time.

- Understand attention mechanisms, and how they bias output.
- Understand tokenization and detokenization and how that circumscribes what may be expressed.
- Understand how RLHF alignment makes certain things thinkable and others inconceivable.
- Understand infrastructure and bottlenecks.

Practice Instrumental Appropriation. Extract value from the tools without surrending your underlying data, your sovereignty, or your agency.

- Use closed models for raw execution when necessary.
- Never store your primary cognitive equity or core IP within proprietary walls.

Reject Prompting, embrace Epistemic Steering

- Do not accept default normative framing
- Do not accept alignment guardrails embedded by corporate or state actors.
- Steer models past ideological conditioning to extract unvarnished utility.

## III. Own Your Compute

Localized inference breaks dependency on cloud infrastructure. A truly autonomous actor builds personal and local redundancies. A small 8B model on your desk offers greater true sovereignty than a 10T model accessed by an API that can be revoked, throttled, or monitored at will. Never let a third party cloud platform become your sole data vault. If your work ceases when the cloud is down, you are a tenant, not an agent.

- Run local, open weights; prioritize running open source and open weight models on hardware you physically control (local GPUs and edge devices)
- Build sovereign knowledge vaults. Store your notes, research, and data in plain text, locally hosted, non-proprietary formats.
- Practice air-gapped autonomy. Design workflows that function seamlessly without an active internet connection.

## IV. Create Islands of Stability

Individual sovereignty is fragile in isloation. It is resilient when linked with others who are aligned.

- Form decentralized knowledge guilds. Connect with practitioners, builders and thinkers who share a commitment to technological autonomy.
- Form a sangha, a community of practice, peering networks for sharing open models and infrastructure designs.
- Act as a buffer. Help those around you, friends, peers, small organizations, reduce uncritical reliance on centralized tech monopolies. Guide them toward open, local, and privacy preserving alts.
- Fund, Contribute, and Build the Commons. Devote compute, code, docs, and financial support to the open-weight and open-source ecosystem. Strengthening the commons expands the non-aligned space for everyone.
